Attribution

  • Mod is the crashing code only if its managed type or Burst/native DLL is on the faulting stack.
  • Handled managed errors on another thread are not the crash site.
  • cohtml.WindowsDesktop.dll, cohtml.Net, VCRUNTIME140.dll are game/runtime.
  • A mod can still provoke a vanilla native bug without appearing on the stack. That is not proven by stack absence, and cannot be proven without Cohtml source or a repro.
